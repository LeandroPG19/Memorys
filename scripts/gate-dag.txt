# scripts/gate-dag.txt: the rows of the judge, one per line (CI-F3, D-F3a, D-F3b, CI-F3.3b-2).
# <idx> <id> <deps> <recursos> <clase> <cpu> <ram_gb> <dur_s> <orden…>
# idx: the serial order of the judge, multiples of 10; merge-gate.sh runs the rows in it. deps: ids of this table, or '-'.
# recursos: target:T, cluster:C, models, comma separated, or '-'. clase: normal, exclusive, boundary or untimed.
# untimed (CI-F3.3b-2): the row schedules as normal, but its output is past the boundary line the judge of the test
# times reads up to (the planner prints the line before the first untimed block): only CV and M are untimed.
# criterio de cpu y ram: the most cpu and ram that the lanes of a row ask for in merge-gate.sh (cpu of the heaviest
# lane of its chain, from the table before CI-F3.3b-2; P, DC, CV and M take the figures of the steps they run).
# dur_s: the starting duration of a row without history (ci-rapido-F3.spec.md, F3.3b-1b-ii); with history the planner
# takes the median of its last runs.
# The order of a row is its step: `bash scripts/merge-gate.sh --step <id>`, and merge-gate.sh step_<id>() holds the work.
