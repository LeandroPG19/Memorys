# 0.30 — what 0.29 left on purpose

Branch `feat/0.30-robustez`, from `c4b95dc` (= `origin/main` = `v0.29.0`).

0.29.0 shipped with every item below known, judged not blocking and written down (the 0.29 backlog,
sections «Seen by W8/W9», «Seen by W11» and «Open, carried»). 0.30 is the batch that closes the ones
that do **not** need a real host. The method is `trabajo-en-lotes`: the whole list first, every slice
specified and implemented together, one `todo` green over the final SHA, then ship, then harden.

## Order and parallelism

1. Specifiers, four alive at most: K1, K2, K3, T1. Then T2.
2. Implementers, three at most, each in its own worktree, tests first (`test(rojo)` commit), then code.
   K1, K2 and K3 touch different files and go together; T1 and T2 touch `rust/tests/` and go after them.
3. G1, G2, G3 run in series, after the product slices: a change to the judge invalidates the receipt of
   everything above it.
4. `todo` once over the final SHA on Windows and on Linux, then `quality-gate`, then `release.sh v0.30.0`.

### What the measurements corrected

- **G2.** `cargo deny` and `cargo audit` do not share the advisory database, and the gate's `deny` does not check advisories: `audit` is the only judge of them. The defect was that it ran twenty minutes in with no ceiling.
- **T2.** A dead database at `127.0.0.1:1` costs 5 s on Linux too: sqlx retries a refused connection until `acquire_timeout`. `v026`'s 120 s were the reranker warm-up of each daemon it starts, not a client waiting in the backlog.
- **G3.** cargo-mutants 27.1.0 does not apply `.gitignore` to its copy here, with or without `--gitignore=false`; the disk went on 25.8 GB of foreign `target-*` directories inside `rust/` copied once per job.
