# 0.30 — what 0.29 left on purpose

Branch `feat/0.30-robustez`, from `c4b95dc` (= `origin/main` = `v0.29.0`).

0.29.0 shipped with every item below known, judged not blocking and written down (the 0.29 backlog,
sections «Seen by W8/W9», «Seen by W11» and «Open, carried»). 0.30 is the batch that closes the ones
that do **not** need a real host. The method is `trabajo-en-lotes`: the whole list first, every slice
specified and implemented together, one `todo` green over the final SHA, then ship, then harden.

## Not in this release, and why

| Item | Why it waits |
|---|---|
| M1/M2, the host's two reboots; «`bridge doctor` reads *tiempo agotado*»; the `.env` found from the scheduled task | They need the real host (madre) and a client PC. Whatever they show is 0.31. |
| F4, migrate the company machine | Needs that machine. |
| R8, TLS on the LAN bind | A project, not a slice (decided in 0.27). |
| Compacting the WSL and Docker virtual disks (~300 GB of C:) | Needs the user's UAC click and WSL + Docker stopped. Not code. |
| `TimeoutLayer` behavioural test | Its seam is a new environment variable; declared hole since 0.27. |

Every slice goes through the six-pack. «Before launching, check the code and `git log`»: the specifier's
first job is to confirm the slice is still open (on 2026-09-30 two of eleven were already done).

## Order and parallelism

1. Specifiers, four alive at most: K1, K2, K3, T1. Then T2.
2. Implementers, three at most, each in its own worktree, tests first (`test(rojo)` commit), then code.
   K1, K2 and K3 touch different files and go together; T1 and T2 touch `rust/tests/` and go after them.
3. G1, G2, G3 run in series, after the product slices: a change to the judge invalidates the receipt of
   everything above it.
4. `todo` once over the final SHA on Windows and on Linux, then `quality-gate`, then `release.sh v0.30.0`.

## Gate for this release

`./scripts/merge-gate.sh` over the final SHA on both hosts, exit 0 with a clean log (one judge at a time
across the two hosts, `docs/gate.md`); then `./scripts/quality-gate.sh` for the diff; then
`./scripts/release.sh v0.30.0`. The user's order is needed to publish.
